# DealOrNoThankYou


## How to Play

### Starting the Game
Run the DealOrNoThankYou.java file.

Click the Start button.

The game begins with 26 sealed cases.
Each case has a randomly assigned cash value.
All possible cash values can be seen on the right side of the panel.


### Round 1:

The player selects one case that they will keep possession of until the final ### Round of the game.


### Round 2:

The player selects 6 cases to eliminate from play.
After selecting each case, the contents of that case is revealed and that value is grayed out from the panel.


### Round 3: 

The banker makes a cash offer to purchase the case that the player selected in ### Round 1.
The player can choose to accept the offer and be awarded the cash offer from the banker.
If the player declines the offer, they continue to the next round.


### Round 4:

The player selects 5 cases to eliminate from play.
After selecting each case, the contents of that case is revealed and that value is grayed out from the panel.

### Round 8:

### Round 9:

### Round 19:

The banker makes a cash offer to purchase the case that the player selected in ### Round 1.
The player can choose to accept the offer and be awarded the cash offer from the banker.
If the player declines the offer, they continue to the next round.


### Round 20:

In the final round, only the player's case and one unopened case remain.
At this point, the player can choose to keep their case or swap for the case that is still on the board.
Once the decision is made, it is revealed how much cash is inside the chosen case and the game is over.
